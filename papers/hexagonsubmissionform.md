Title: Explicit Involution Geometry of Product Kummer Surfaces

Authors, in published order:

Abstract: We classify the 31 nonidentity involutions in the standard affine two-torsion subgroup of a product Kummer surface. We compute their fixed loci, quotient surfaces and rational cohomology representations, including the fixed loci of all fifteen translations descending to each of the nine Lieberman quotients.

Keywords: K3 surfaces; Kummer surfaces; Enriques surfaces; involutions
