Version 2 has been retitled Explicit Involution Geometry of Product Kummer Surfaces to identify its precise scope. It replaces the earlier general discussion with complete fixed-locus proofs for the standard 32-element affine two-torsion subgroup, the corresponding quotient identifications and the descended translation action on each Lieberman quotient. It adds rational cohomology representations, local resolution calculations, new diagrams, appendices and exact finite checks. The Fermat quartic example is corrected to a symplectic involution with eight fixed points; general Nikulin quotients are no longer asserted to be Kummer, and the projectivity and lattice statements have been corrected. Classical constructions are credited and the nine free group elements are distinguished from a classification of all Enriques quotients. The published author order and corresponding author are unchanged.
